# Company, Inc. Term Sheets

An interactive study guide to a four-round venture financing (Series A through D). It explains each key term, the exceptions and why they matter, with a comparison across rounds.

Live: https://company-inc-term-sheets.vercel.app

## Tabs

- **Summary**: price, valuation and ownership charts, a side-by-side term table, and what changed between rounds.
- **How it works**: plain-English lessons on valuation and ownership, the option pool, anti-dilution (with a calculator), protective provisions, forced conversion, ROFR and co-sale, and registration rights.
- **Payouts vs exit**: an interactive chart of investor payout against exit value for debt or straight preferred, common, redeemable preferred plus common, convertible and participating preferred, capped participation, multiple liquidation preferences, SAFEs and common with founder vesting, in a sale or an IPO.
- **Series A–D**: each round's terms as expandable cards, with change badges and glossary hovers.

## Notes

A single static `index.html` with no build step. Payout examples use illustrative numbers. Items marked "Interpretation" are the author's reading, not text from the term sheets. This is a learning aid, not legal or investment advice.
